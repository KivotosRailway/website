After being renovated by Maki_Konuri, Qingya Station welcomes passengers today with a brand-new appearance!
The renovation moved Qingya Station westward to connect it to the Sunrise Plains Loop. The platforms were also rearranged and equipped with platform screen doors, among other improvements.
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/1eea36a4f4ddc74075384.png)
The first floor contains the entrances, ticket gates, and platforms for the Holly Line and the South Cross Line toward Songmishan, allowing same-platform transfers. The second floor contains the Sunrise Plains Loop and the South Cross Line toward Shuilin. The inner loop of the Sunrise Plains Loop and the South Cross Line toward Shuilin also support same-platform transfers.
