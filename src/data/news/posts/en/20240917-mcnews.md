On September 17, 2024, the eRAIL North-South Line (Ai Server → Binjiang) underwent a realignment project. Binjiang North Station (formerly Binjiang Administrative Committee Station) and Binjiang Station were rerouted to new platforms. With this, the eRAIL North-South Line reconstruction project was successfully completed, and the groundwork was laid for the planned Phase 4 extension of the eRAIL North-South Line.
The old line and related facilities will be dismantled by Danheng0502 at a later date.
