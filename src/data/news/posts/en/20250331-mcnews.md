The railway monthly report for March 2025 is here~
Since almost all server owners have not built anything this month, it will still be very short.

In March we carried out the following construction:
* Millennium Railway Academy belongs to the rail transit system D.U. Baitiao District Passenger Automatic Transport System and Line 1/2 have started trial operation.
* Xuefei Slow Line (Witch’s Spring-Mixiao) will start its initial operation with the Miaohua connection line.
* Hoshino Electric Railway's Gardenia Line (Kusami-Mizubei) is opened, initial operation begins, and limited express trains from Abydos to Mizubei are launched.
