# Hoshino Main Line
This month, the main works of Hoshino Railway’s Hoshino Main Line Kuzu Station and Ono Station were completed. Let’s take a look at these two stations through photos below.
## Jiuci Station
On December 14, with the completion of the guide layout, Jiuci Station was officially completed. This station is the current southern terminus of the Hoshino Main Line. Local trains and rapid trains depart from different platforms.

## Ono Station
On November 4, the main project of Ono Station was completed. This station is located one station north of Jiuci Station. Only ordinary trains stop, and the entrance and exit are yet to be built.

![](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/12/04/693172b07d28f.png)# Southeast Main Line Renovation
This month, the Southeast Main Line began its second major renovation. This renovation includes returning the coniferous island section to the ground and connecting it to the eastern half of the Sunrise Plain Loop (Sunset Highlands-South Island Central). At the same time, using the structure of the reserved platform of the Sunrise Plain Loop between Sunset Highlands and Suiyun Industrial Park, the Frost Star Highlander Mihua Branch Station was added. The station was completed on November 30. The station hall is above ground and the platform is underground, connecting Mihua and Shuangxing Town. On the north side of the station is the Jacobin Railway Club Training Center (Highland Railway Academy Mihua Branch) under construction. The barrier-free elevator is on the south side of the station. The original Mihua Station of the Super Suburban Railway has also been moved to this station.

#Latest progress of Aokong Line
At the end of this month, the latest progress was made on the Okora Line, which is part of the Abydos regional rail system. The Chitose Coordinate Tower-Countermeasures Center section has been connected, and construction is also underway at Yasuura Station located in the middle of this section.
