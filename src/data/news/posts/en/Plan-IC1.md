## Overview
This project includes the deep intercity (Pujiang end-Dongshan) and Miyou intercity (Miyoudao-Pujiang end). The deep intercity section is upgraded from the original Jacobin deep line (Comet Express Line D), and the Miyou intercity section is a newly built section. The maximum speed is 200km/h.
