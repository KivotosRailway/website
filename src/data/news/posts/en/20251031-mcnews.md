# Xuefei Slow Line
In October, the final section of the Bastogne-Pujiang section of the Sherfield Slow Line (including the parallel section A of the Comet Express) completed line reconstruction.
The height of the road section west of the Baipu River was reduced to ease the slope. At the same time, the Shalingzi triangle area was rebuilt, and two pairs of crossings were added to connect the tracks of the Snowfield Slow Line and the Comet Express Line A.

#New wayfinding system
This month, Hoshino Railway introduced a brand new wayfinding system, which has been put into use at Texel Station, Abydos Station and Chitose Coordinate Tower Station. With clear visual design and efficient functionality as its core, this guidance system provides passengers with an intuitive navigation experience through concise and clear line markings and direction guidance. The system uses bilingual markings in Chinese and English, combined with distinctive color coding, to effectively help passengers quickly locate and travel to their destinations. The overall layout is orderly and the visual presentation is modern and simple, creating a comfortable and efficient traffic environment, which fully reflects the humanity and convenience of modern transportation facilities.
