At 0:00 on October 2, 2024, an exciting news came - the "Comet Lake Express" was officially established! This name was voted by the group members, symbolizing the formal establishment of the rail transit express system of this server. The Comet Express is an important part of the rail transit resource integration plan proposed by the Jacobin Club. It will reintegrate multiple original express lines in the server to form a unified express network composed of six lines.
* Express Line A (Urawa Hanako-Genrinfu): Integrated from Nitta Line A and Maki Electric Railway Ajidani Line
* Express Line B (Moramanker Town-Hyland): Former Xintian Line B
* Express Line C (Hokkaido-South Island Central): Former Jacobin Central Line
* Express Line D (Pujiangmo-Ningzhou): Original Jacobin Depth Line
* Express Line E (Mongyuze Free City-South Island Central): Former Maki Electric Railway Hoshie Line
* Express Line F (Southbank Center-Moramanker Town): Former Tradewinds Metro Furukawa Line
![Comet Express Logo](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/04/02/67ece50d760f8.png)
![Latest route map](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/04/02/67ece4ae154f8.png)

According to the Jacobin Club, the launch of the Comet Express marks a new beginning. After this, the Jacobin Railway Club, which originally owned two lines, will become an organization composed of various railway companies, focusing on the management of railways and promoting cooperation among operators. At the same time, a new Jacobin Railway Club building will be built to the north of the KR headquarters (rebirth point), which will be more conducive to the unification of resources. At the same time, the third floor will be used as part of the new station hall of the Comet Express Line D Jacobin Club Station. At present, Comet Express has settled in the club building, and the club building will be expanded in the future to play a greater role.
![The brand new Jacobin Club administrative building, now Comet Express has moved in](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/04/02/67ece44dddd9e.png)
This integration plan not only improves the operational efficiency of rail transit, but also brings new vitality to the transportation network within the server. With the establishment of Comet Express, future rail transit development will be more collaborative and sustainable, providing passengers with a better travel experience.
