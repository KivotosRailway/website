After six long months of construction, Highland Station is finally ready to open and welcomes passengers today!
![Highland Main Line information card](https://s21.ax1x.com/2024/09/20/pAMSLRJ.png)
The station is on the Highland Main Line, which provides access to Froststar Town and Broken Cloud Industrial Park. The Highland Railway Orange Line and Shinden B Line will also connect here in the future!
A road to Froststar Town is currently being planned.
