On October 1, 2024, half a year after the plan was proposed, the Dreamy Lake Free City extension of the Underground Exurban Line finally began operation, providing local residents and visitors with a more convenient transportation option and marking another milestone in public transit development.
This extension opened a single new station: Dreamy Lake Free City Station. Its opening not only shortens the distance between the suburbs and the city center, but also injects new vitality into the economic development of the surrounding area.
![Dreamy Lake Free City Station entrance](https://s21.ax1x.com/2024/10/04/pA81gB9.png)
At the same time, Lumi_Nosity announced that a large-scale development plan will be carried out in this area, and we will continue to follow the progress.
