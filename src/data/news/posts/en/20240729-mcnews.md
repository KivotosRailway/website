On July 29, 2024, the new Mihua Station—formed by merging the former Mihua and Froststar stations—officially entered service. The renovation transformed the two simple stations into a spacious, bright enclosed station where passengers can clearly experience the change from snowfield to plain.

The platform of the former Mihua Station has been closed.

A photo spot has been set up near the northeast entrance for passengers to take commemorative pictures.
