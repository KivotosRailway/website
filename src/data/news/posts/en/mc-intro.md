## Setting introduction
### Jacobin Club
The Jacobin Club is the administrative organization of this server, and its subordinates include the Ministry of Transport, the Ministry of Urban Development, and the Jacobin Railway Club.
The Jacobin Railway Club belongs to the Jiaotong Department and manages various railway companies.
![Jacobin Railway Club Logo](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/666f4a4f90712268f55d0.png)
### MAKI GROUP
The parent company of Maki Electric Railway, Eastern Railway (eRAIL) and Tunnel Rail Transit (each operates independently), with its headquarters in Maki Park (next to the Hakuryu River).
![MAKI GROUP Logo](https://s21.ax1x.com/2024/12/19/pAO8xoT.png)## Some views
