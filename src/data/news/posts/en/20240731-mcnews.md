With the reserved interface at Chuiyun Industrial Park Station connected to the railway, the remaining section of the KoriKOS Transport loop line—the Sunrise Plains Loop Line (Chuiyun Industrial Park–South Island Central)—has officially opened, marking the beginning of full loop operation for the Sunrise Plains Loop Line.
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/ebfa1f8badf331dcb9f96.png)
This opening added two new stations: Minamishima East Station and Shiroko Island Station (with Hoshinzan Station still pending). Let’s take a closer look!
## Shiroko Island Station
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/8f3f21c092d5b0d205dbd.png)
Located on a small island in the southern part of Qingya, this station makes it easier to relax, camp, or enjoy some combat gameplay when you have time in the story.
## Minamishima East Station
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/0faed7d950d13ac6a9857.png)
Located on the sea to the northeast of South Island, it is currently open only for scenic viewing and does not allow passengers to disembark.
