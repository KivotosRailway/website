The railway monthly report for February 2025 is here~
However, due to lack of time this time, this monthly report will be very brief.

In February we carried out the following construction:
* Binjiang Channel (Binjiang-Xinxia, formerly eRAIL north-south line reconstruction), a key part of the Xiaogou Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Binjiang Line system, has completed preliminary construction. From here, BRT can pass through two major industrial parks—Maki Park and Hokkaido Industrial Park, and finally Xinxia.
* The eastern extension of the New Port Line (KR Headquarters-Haewon) and the western extension (Baipura North-Texel) were initially put into operation, forming a west-southeast artery.
* The Fangbai Intercity was renamed the Comet Express Line D, and the Texel-D.U. Central Station was opened.
* Comet Express Line B (KR Headquarters-Buyocaowo) is now open.
