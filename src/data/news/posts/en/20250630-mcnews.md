# New progress in the modernization project of the Snowfield Slow Line
On June 12, 2025, one of the key projects of this service - the modernization project of the Sherfield Slow Line - ushered in an important moment: the track sections of the entire line have been modernized and test operations have begun. Next, each station will be gradually transformed to meet modern requirements and better serve passengers.
The current Sheffield slowdown line is divided into three parts:
* Main line (Pujiang end-Ningzhou): Branch off from the Shenye branch line at Shalingzi Station, and the western section is expected to extend to the Abydos area.
* Shenye branch line (Shenye-Shalingzi): connected with the main line, serving the local people in Shenye.
* Bauhinia branch line (Zijingshan-Shenye): regional connection line, and directly connected to Tadano depot.
