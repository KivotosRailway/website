#hollyline
In August, the Dongqing Line renovation project made good progress:
* On August 3, the Dongqing Line at Dongshan Station was partially completed.
* On August 15, the reconstruction of Shuilin Station was completed (together with the Xingang Line part and the Nanheng Line part)
* On August 20, the renovation of Friendship Port Station was completed (together with the New Port Line part)

In August, the modernization of the Snowfield Slow Line also made progress, with the completion of the reconstruction of the following three stations:
## Honma Himawari Station
On August 10, the first phase of the Honma Himawari Station reconstruction project was completed. This project included the reconstruction of the Snowfield Slow Line section, adopting an island-style design of one line and two lines, and a platform for the new line was reserved above.
The second phase of the project to be carried out later will carry out the construction of the New Port Line part and connect it with the existing part.

## Pujiang terminal station
On August 26, the construction of the Uejiang Terminal Station (part of the Snowfield Slow Line) adjacent to the southwest of Honma Himawari Station was also completed.
This station adopts a design of 2 island platforms + 1 side platform with 4 lines. The side platform (in the direction of Ningzhou) is directly connected to the deep intercity station concourse, making it convenient for passengers who get off the intercity train to directly transfer to the Sheffield Slow Line train heading towards the city. It is also convenient for passengers to go to Barstow Station to transfer to the Comet Express Line A and the Suburban Line.

## Shalingzi Station
On August 15, the renovation of Shalingzi Station, a branch station of the Xuefei Slow Line, was completed.
This renovation significantly renovated the exterior and also brought a broad view.
The entrance and exit in the original structure are retained on the northwest side of the station as a commemoration.
