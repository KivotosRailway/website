The railway monthly report for May 2025 is here~

#New progress of Comet Express Line B
This month, Comet Express Line B has advanced northward to the Mengyuze area.
However, this month there will be plans for the Mengyuze area and Yuhuaze in the north, which will also involve adjustments to the Express Line B plan.
At the same time, the station hall platforms of Express Line B and Express Line D at KR Headquarters Station have also been greatly renovated to facilitate the follow-up construction of Express Line B.

# New Taipei Line Tadano Station
This month, the Xingang Metro New Taipei Line Depot Connection Line (Kita-Tadano Depot-Shiraura Kita) was completed. The Tadano Station supporting the project was also built by Cucit2048 as the permanent terminal station at the northwest end of the New Taipei Line.

# Hoshino Main Line’s 1+1>2
This month, Hoshino Railway's Hoshino Main Line and Shiroko Line were integrated and officially operated as one line, with the southern end also extending to Kuzu.

![Hoshino Main Line Phase 3 Southern Section](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/06/07/684319a2a4024.png)

# South Island Central Station renovation is in progress
South Island Central Station has also undergone renovations due to the construction of the South Island Express and the main line extension of the Holly Line. This month, the renovation of the Sunrise Plains Circle Line platform and concourse has been completed.
![](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/06/07/684315911ed76.png)
![](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/06/07/68431599388e4.png)

#Jacobin Club Support Project
This month, the Jacobin Club also carried out two support projects in remote villages - the office located east of Zhenqin Town and the Nanhan Ming Beacon, trying to bring new development opportunities to these villages.
