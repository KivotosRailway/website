Note: The following plans may change beyond recognition at any time

In order to cooperate with Aixzusa Shirasu1 to build the Nitta Line A and promote the development of the western region, the western railway plan is now announced:
Plan subject:
* Nitta Line A Phase 1: KR Headquarters → Urawa Hanako (Reeve) (this line has been merged into Comet Express Line A)

Supporting lines:
* West extension of the Maki Electric Railway Snowline Slow Line: Bastogne → Rokko Loop/Honma Himawari (as the slow line of Nitta Line A)
* Phase 4 of the Maki Electric Railway Heart Summer Line: Snowfall → Divine Industry
* West extension of the New Port Line: Shiraura Kita → Honma Himawari → Ejiak → Rotimer
* The second phase of the Jacobin depth line: Urae end → Ejiak/Rokko East (selected) → Urawa Hanako (Rif) (directly connected to Nitta Line A, and the depth line will be changed to the right)
