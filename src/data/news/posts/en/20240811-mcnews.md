With the connections at Shanjiahu Station and AI Server Station linked to the eRAIL East-West Line in succession, the eRAIL East-West Line has officially joined Maki Railway's Sheffy Local Line. From now on, the two lines will operate as one, providing passengers with more convenient train services.
Related projects—including station renovations, replacement of wayfinding signs, and double-tracking the single-track section from Shanjiahu to Ningzhou—will also proceed gradually.
* Trains currently traveling to and from the Jacobin Club are temporarily connected to the former eRAIL East-West Line tracks. Once the new platform at Jacobin Club Station is completed, the line will be switched to the new platform.
