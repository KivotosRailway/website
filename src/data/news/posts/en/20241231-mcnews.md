First of all, Kivotos Railway Company wishes everyone a Happy New Year! Welcome to the KR Railway monthly report!
In December, our rail transit has entered a new era, let us take a look!
## Bomin Line Sighmen Station & Modernization Project
On December 27, the modernization project of the Bomin Line and the Sighmen Station were completed and opened to traffic, providing better train services to the region.
At the same time, the Sigh Gate will also be built into a new transportation hub to strengthen connections and personnel exchanges with the lower world.

![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/6813832e5fe27.png)## Comet Express Line A is fully open
On December 28, a much-anticipated line was finally opened to traffic. It is the Comet Express Line A! Its significance is very huge, not only in connecting the western region, the northeastern region and the city center, but also in many aspects. Comet Express Line A, as the pioneering work of express line modernization, finally achieved a breakthrough from 0 to 1 after nine months, which also means that the first phase plan has been implemented except for the fourth phase of the eRAIL North and South Line (the sightseeing loop line has been cancelled).

On the day of the opening, an opening ceremony was also held at the KR headquarters station. According to reports from the God of Change, old people, children, men and women, brave demon kings, various creatures from other worlds and even groups calling themselves Mitta came to the scene to celebrate this historic moment.
Let’s take a look at some of the highlights along the route~
### Qifute Town Station
The station located in the western mainland was built under the town of Qifute managed by Aaron212. It is configured as a side type with 2 stations and 4 lines. The station's decoration cleverly blends into the town's architectural style.

### KR Headquarters Station Renovation
In order to be able to access the Comet Express Line A and optimize the transfer flow, the KR Headquarters Station has undergone major renovations. Three bright transfer halls have been opened on the east and west sides of the station, improving the transfer of the Sherfield Slow Line, Comet Express Line A/C and Newport Line.## Brand new heart summer line
After a series of connection projects carried out by Misaka Akio, the Shinka Line officially took over the entire line of the Comet Express Line F (formerly Furukawa Line), and its operating scope changed to Sinzhou-Moramanlk Town. The modern K-Train with the logos of Maki Electric Railway and Shinka Line can go directly from the city center to the snowfields, adding more vitality to the transportation between the two places.
