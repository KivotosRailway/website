On July 28, 2024, a new system entered service across the Jacobin Central Line. Let's follow our very fast reporter and take a look!
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/6d8b180ac44b2f7ad7f34.png)
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/af0316ebf2f98620a795d.png)
It is a minecart generator. Clicking it automatically creates a minecart, which departs after 10 seconds, so passengers no longer need to bring their own cart to use rail transit. After getting off, the minecart is cleared automatically—no more worrying about empty carts blocking the tracks!
