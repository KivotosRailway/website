Today marks an important transition for the Sheffy Local Line: Shanjiahu Station has completed its southward relocation and now serves passengers with new platforms and a new concourse.
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/05a5e10e839e3fe09dd21.png)
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/05d8ad830fc1110577a53.png)
Our fast-running reporter also discovered two reserved connections in the turnback area beyond the station. According to Maki Railway president Maki_Konuri, once the time is right, a project will merge the eRAIL East-West Line into the Sheffy Local Line. The project aims to optimize the line layout, improve service quality, establish unified management, and connect the two lines in all four directions, making travel more convenient. The project to double-track the single-track section of the eRAIL East-West Line will also proceed steadily.
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/e487af3f62ed644ff8455.png)
Meanwhile, the tracks at the former Shanjiahu Station have been removed. The station structure will be used as an observation deck, allowing visitors to enjoy a panoramic view of Shanjiahu.
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/2aeb5d2d422a3ca7a760d.png)
