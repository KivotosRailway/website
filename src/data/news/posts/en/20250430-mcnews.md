#New eRAIL and New North-South Line
On April 26, 2025, the third APM line in this server—the Eastern Railway eRAIL (new) North-South Line officially opened! This line evolved from the Mihua connecting line (Witch's Spring-Mihua) and the Star Line (Singzhou-Xingguang Mountain). As a tangent, it provides the southeastern plains with a quick link between the Sherfield Line and Sin Chew.
![North-South Line Information Card](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/05/02/681459baa6ec4.png)
On the first day of operation of this line, a large number of passengers came to experience it. One of the passengers, Trala Iero Tralala, specially logged in from the coast near Sin Chew and experienced the new North-South Line. He said that the train runs very smoothly and is fast, which saves much effort compared to traveling by himself!
The core station of this line, Libra Marsh Station, is not only directly connected to Shanshui Huating, providing more choices for local residents, but a new East-West Line (also an APM line) will be introduced in the future.
