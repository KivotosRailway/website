In the first issue of route introduction, we will focus on one of the Four Heavenly Kings of the server - Highlander Main Line (formerly Aomori Main Line)!
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/6813876b2f852.png)
It was built by former members during the server opening period and opened the KR Headquarters-Aomori section, which was later extended to Aomori New Town (now Memorial Station). It will be extended from Aaron 212 to the center of the South Island on March 19, 2024.
On March 20, 2024, the South Coast Contact Line, led by Maki_Konuri and assisted by Aaron212, was completed and opened to traffic, realizing direct operation with the Jacobin Central Line.
Subsequently, under the leadership of Maki_Konuri, various modifications and extensions of the line were carried out.## Station introduction
### KR Headquarters
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/681387f142bc5.png)
It is located underground in the center of the world (the Rebirth Area), passing through the Jacobin Central Line (now Comet Express Line C) from below, and the west platform is connected to the Newport Line platform through a passage.
A package of renovations including platform elevation will be completed on July 18, 2024, and it has now been taken over by Xintian Line A.
### Simon Shaw
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138822d16df.png)
Located underground in a town in the west of the world's center (Rebirth Area), it has complete internal facilities. The station concourse connects the Snowfield Slow Line and the Nitta Line A platform (currently the only way to get to the platform).
A station-front crossing line (now demolished) was added on July 18, 2024, and is now taken over by the Xintian A line.
### Bicun Ranch
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/6813885b8135e.png)
Located under the mountains south of Simonshaw, it was handed over to the Snowfield Slow Line with the completion of the Simonshaw Contact Line on March 22, 2024, and is currently inactive.
### South Bank Center
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138906ceeaf.png)
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/6813890719913.png)
A small island located in the south of the world's most central area (Rebirth Zone), currently only provides transfers to the Sheffield Slow Line and the Jacobin Central Line.
The station has 2 platforms and 4 lines, the outer 2 lines are handed over to the Sherfield Slow Line, and the Highlander Line uses the inner 2 lines.
### Xiaoyao Gangnan
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/6813892f55f80.png)
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138932c4a87.png)
Formerly known as Tea Garden Station, it is located on the south side of Xiaoyaogang Industrial Zone, across Xili Lake, and can be transferred to the Star Line to reach the industrial zone.
The South Bank Contact Line leads to the west side of the station, through which you can directly connect to the Jacobin Central Line (it is connected to the Jacobin Central Line South Bank Center platform)
### Sunset Heights
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/681389ae1127d.png)
Reserved station located south of Sunset Plateau.
### Frost Star (Freight Station)
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/681389ff0c010.png)
The former Aomori Station is a station located in the center of town and is currently used as a freight station.
### Hyland
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138a24110b3.png)
The hub station after the original Aomori Station was moved south will open on August 2, 2024, and has now been merged with Suiyun Industrial Park Station.
### Memorial
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138a4ca5f3b.png)
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138a4f1a669.png)
The original Aomori New Town Station is located in Lingyang Village in the south of Highlander, and can be transferred to the Nanyoko Line and Hoshie Line.
### Central South Island
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138a6feb981.png)
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138a69c10b1.png)
The station on the South Island shares a platform with the Hoshii Line and can be transferred to the Sunrise Plains Loop Line.
### Feng He
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138a8b8b444.png)
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138a8d71fd7.png)
The new station will be opened on May 22, 2024. Above the station is a house named "Hakuzhou Azusa", which belongs to RouisNagisa.
### Snowfall
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138aa655246.png)
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138aa87f66d.png)
The new station, which will open on May 22, 2024, can be interchanged with the authentic suburban line.## Future planning

## Others
### Nanhai Bridge
The cable-stayed bridge built by Aaron212 was completed on March 18, 2024.
![](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/05/01/68138abd58742.png)
### Early Access Plan
Before the South Bank Contact Line, there was a direct connection plan between the main line and the Jacobin Central Line. When it was originally built, it was planned to establish a direct connection through Yudong Station southeast of the South Bank Center, but the plan was later cancelled.
