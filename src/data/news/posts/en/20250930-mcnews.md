# Xuefei Slow Line
In September, the progress of the modernization of the Snowfield Slow Line is as follows:
## KR Headquarters Station
On September 3, the reconstruction project of KR Headquarters Station (part of the Snowfield Slow Line) was completed. This is currently the largest renovation project of the headquarters, integrating the station with the rebirth point building, and the Sherfield Slow Line will also become one of the most important facades.
The KR Headquarters Station on the Sherfield Slow Line uses overlapping platforms to separate trains in two directions. A transfer platform is set up on the middle mezzanine and is directly connected to the rebirth point. A slope is used to connect the transfer platform and the yesterday, which effectively saves space.
![2025-09-03_23.36.10.png](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/10/01/68dcc69bccb1c.png)
## Shuangxing Branch Line (Green Bridge-Suiyun Industrial Park) Rail Link
On September 26, the Xuefei Slow Line Shuangxing Branch (Green Bridge-Suiyun Industrial Park) was put into operation, laying a solid foundation for subsequent projects.
The Shuangxing branch line leads from Green Bridge Station, passing through Nan'an Center, Xiaoyao Gangnan, Sunset Heights, Suiyun Industrial Park, and finally ends at Memorial Station. The platform of the South Bank Center is located directly above the Xinxia Line, with a station hall in the middle for transfers. The latter line is connected to the original Highlander Line and modernized.
