As one of the important stations on the eastern bank of the Bailong River, AI Server Station has welcomed passengers with a brand-new appearance after a major renovation, bringing new vitality to the eastern bank's development. After visiting, the alien god of change remarked: “OZO, ULU.” (Translation: “More changeable than me.”)
The new AI Server Station has four levels:
* 1F Lower Concourse
* 2F Sheffy Local Line
* 3F eRAIL North-South Line and Ultra-Exurban Railway
* 4F Upper Concourse
The station is equipped with four elevators. Two stop at levels 1, 2, and 4 (inside the paid area, connecting to the Sheffy Local Line platforms); one stops at levels 3 and 4 (inside the paid area, connecting to the eRAIL North-South Line platforms); and the final outdoor elevator stops at levels 1, 3, and 4 (connecting the Ultra-Exurban Railway area, upper concourse, and ground level).
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/482d88b7290048c422298.png)
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/ae99acc1f45ad4dded24c.png)
The new eRAIL North-South Line platforms make the most of the available space, achieving as much platform width as possible in a very confined area.
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/fadceabf66f76d9782e26.png)
The dedicated Ultra-Exurban Railway area is right next to them. It is separated from the line while maintaining safety.
![](https://image.kivotosrailway.com/file/d32496c73cee1f0fe43e5.png)
The spacious, bright upper concourse was formerly the eRAIL East-West Line area. Its all-glass design gives passengers a warm feeling.
