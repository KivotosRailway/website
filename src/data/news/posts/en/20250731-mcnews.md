# Liujia East-Pujiangmo-Ajiake transfer channel
In the early morning of August 1, 2025, the transfer passage connecting Rokko East Station, Pujiang Terminal Station and Aijake Station was completed and officially put into use during the day. The completion of this underground passage enabled seamless transfers in the paid area between the three stations. Passengers no longer need to exit the station to the ground floor, effectively avoiding the inconvenience of transfer in hot weather in summer. The transfer channel has clear movement lines and separates the transfer passenger flow in three directions. The transfer efficiency is improved through careful organization.

# Xuefei Slow Line Renovation Progress
This month, as the Snowfield Slow Line continues its trial operation, new progress has been made in the renovation of the stations:
## Shenjiahu Station
The renovation of this station was completed on July 5, 2025, and the original above-ground station was changed to an underground station.

## Sasaki Uehara Station
The renovation of this station was completed on July 19, 2025. It was originally a station on the original railway. During the modernization process, the principle of scale control was adhered to and a streamlined design was adopted, which not only fully meets the needs of use, but also naturally blends into the surrounding landscape.
This station is also on display at the Northern Union Railway Exhibition.

![](https://r2-image.kivotosrailway.com/2025/08/09/6896e07585923.png)# Deep Intercity Pujiang Terminal Station
Due to the renovation this month, the main structure construction of the deep intercity Pujiang terminal station will be completed on July 19, 2025.
This station is the bifurcation node of the deep intercity main line and the Miyoudao branch line. Based on the first occupied station, appropriate improvements have been made while retaining the original architectural features. The overall visual color of Zhongshen Intercity's iconic dark red is used as the main visual color, paired with pure white and bright station hall space, creating a solemn and elegant yet modern transportation hub atmosphere. The red and white color combination not only reflects the continuity of the line, but also enhances spatial recognition through bright tonal contrast.
