## A new era of transportation in Corricaus
On January 15, 2025, with the completion of the renovation of Sunset Heights Station and the modernization project of the Southeast Main Line, this line, one of the Four Heavenly Kings, will also write a new chapter. After the transformation, the Southeast Main Line will provide free services to passengers at 2-minute intervals from 8:00 to 1:00 the next day.

## Hoshino Railway Debut
This month we welcome a brand new railway company - Hoshino Railway. It took over the Snowfall-South Coast Central Section and Highlander-South Island Central Section of the original Highlander Main Line and carried out modernization to become part of the Hoshino Main Line and Shiroko Line respectively. On January 16, the first phase of the Shiroko Line and the first phase of the Hoshino Main Line were opened. The two lines are interconnected, connecting Bonan in the south and emerging cities in the northwest, and also provide strong impetus for the development of the South Bank sub-center.
At the same time, this line is also the first express line in the server. Only stops at Abydos, Neptune, Texel, Rokko East (New Gate), Snowfall, South Bank Centre, Memorial, Bonan.
![Hoshino Railway Logo](https://assets.kivotos.cc/image/2025/01/14/6785481e73832.png)## Modern Xingang Metro
The wind of modernization has also reached Xingang Metro. On January 28, the entire Xingang Line (KR Headquarters - Baipu North) completed the modernization, and 4 mini M-Trains provide services to passengers at 2-minute intervals. The next day, the New Taipei Line also started test operation. These two things mean that Newport Map has entered the modern era in great strides.
In addition, on February 15, the pilot section of the east extension of the Xingang Line (KR Headquarters-Binjiang North) was opened, and the test operation range was changed to the "double-north" operation range from Baipu North to Binjiang North.

## Xiaohou Trading was established
This month, omagari_hare established Xiaogou Trading, dedicated to the development of server bus services.
At present, the rapid transit line B1 of Xiaogou Bus has begun test operation, and the construction of the station is also in full swing. It makes full use of part of the original Highlander Line and part of the roadbed of the fourth phase of the eRAIL North-South Line to provide convenient services for surrounding residents. Lines B2 and B3, which will utilize the existing sections of eRAIL's north-south line, are also being planned.
