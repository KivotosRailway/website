## Kokona Line Xingzhou Extension Opens
On September 2, 2024, as the Kokona Line train slowly entered Xingzhou Station, the Xingzhou extension of the Kokona Line (Kokona → Xingzhou) officially opened. Traveling to Xingzhou no longer requires a transfer to the Holly Line; it is a direct one-train ride. This extension is expected to drive further development in the Kokona and Xingzhou areas.
At the same time, due to the use of a new third-floor platform at Kokona Station on the Kokona Line, the original Kokona → Moshulida section was temporarily suspended.

## These Routes Are Free!
Under the strong request of KoriKOS Transport, the Transportation Bureau announced that the following lines are free to ride starting today:
* KoriKOS Transport – Sunrise Plains Loop Line
* KoriKOS Transport – Southeastern Mainline
* San Kong Metro – San Kong Line
* Starstreak Railway – Starstreak Connector
* eRAIL – Holly Line
* eRAIL – Nanheng Line
